# Business Visualisations

All five charts are generated by `scripts/business_visualizations.py` from the
real CoursePulse `orders_5000.csv` and `customers_1000.csv` data. They are saved
as 300 DPI PNG files and share one colour-blind-friendly palette.

The lesson examples mention product lines and marketing spend, but those fields
are not present in this repository. The charts use the closest available,
auditable business relationships and document the adaptation below.

## Palette and accessibility

The palette uses blue, orange, green, red-orange, and purple from a colour-blind
friendly set. Colours are paired with direct labels, legends, line styles, or
annotations, so no insight depends on colour alone. Grid lines are light and
decorative spines are removed to keep attention on the data.

## Charts

### Chart 1: Revenue by Customer Segment

- **Type:** Horizontal bar chart
- **Question:** Which customer segment generated the most valid revenue in the latest 90 days?
- **Labels:** Segment, revenue in dollars, title, and bar values.
- **Annotation:** Calls out the highest-revenue segment.

### Chart 2: Monthly Revenue Trend

- **Type:** Multi-series line chart
- **Question:** How did revenue change over the latest 12 months for the top three segments?
- **Labels:** Month, revenue in dollars, segment legend, and formatted dates.
- **Annotation:** Marks the highest monthly segment value.

### Chart 3: Order Value Distribution

- **Type:** Histogram
- **Question:** What is the distribution of positive order values?
- **Labels:** Order value in dollars, order count, and median legend.
- **Annotation:** Marks the median order value to provide a useful centre reference.

### Chart 4: Quarterly Revenue Composition

- **Type:** Stacked bar chart
- **Question:** How is each quarter's revenue composed across customer segments?
- **Labels:** Quarter, revenue in dollars, segment legend, and total context.
- **Annotation:** Marks the largest quarterly total.

### Chart 5: Customer Orders versus Revenue

- **Type:** Scatter plot with trend line
- **Question:** Is customer order volume associated with customer revenue?
- **Labels:** Orders per customer, customer revenue in dollars, legend, and correlation coefficient.
- **Annotation:** Identifies the highest-revenue customer.

## Run and validate

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/business_visualizations.py
.venv/bin/python -m unittest scripts/test_business_visualizations.py
```

The test suite verifies the source fields and confirms all five PNG files are
created and non-empty.
