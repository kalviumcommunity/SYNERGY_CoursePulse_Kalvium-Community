PER-STAGE RECOMMENDATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

[Sign Up -> Email Entered]
20% of visitors abandon before entering an email. Hypothesis: form is too long or asks for information too early. Action: reduce the initial sign-up form to email-only, gate extra fields behind step 2.

[Email Entered -> Password Created]
25% drop between email and password. Hypothesis: password policy is too strict or confusing. Action: show password strength meter in real-time and allow social/SSO login as an alternative.

[Password Created -> Email Verified]
17% drop at email verification. Hypothesis: verification email lands in spam or takes too long. Action: send instant, branded verification email; add 'resend' button prominently; consider magic-link login instead.

[Email Verified -> Payment Added]
20% drop before adding payment. Hypothesis: asking for payment too early creates friction/distrust. Action: offer a 7-day free trial before requiring payment; add trust signals (SSL badge, money-back guarantee) near the form.

[Payment Added -> First Purchase]
50% drop — the LARGEST revenue leak. Hypothesis: users added payment but never found a reason to buy, OR checkout UX has errors/hidden costs. Action: trigger personalised onboarding email sequence within 1 hour of payment addition; add 'complete your first order' nudge in-app; A/B test removing friction from the checkout page.
